This is the place for things that are too long for a message. When I need to explain something in detail — a write-up of a project, notes from a course, or an announcement — I'll post it here and send you the link instead of a wall of text.

## What you'll find here

- Notes on data analysis and the tools I use day to day
- Write-ups of projects, like the housing pricing work
- Occasionally, some mathematics

Posts can include formulas too. For example, a simple pricing model might start from a linear regression:

$$\hat{p} = \beta_0 + \beta_1 \cdot \text{area} + \beta_2 \cdot \text{age} + \varepsilon$$

Thanks for reading.
